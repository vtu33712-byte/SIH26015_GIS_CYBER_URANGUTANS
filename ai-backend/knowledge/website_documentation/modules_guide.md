# JAL IMPACT Website Modules & Navigation Map

The JAL IMPACT platform includes the following verified routes and operational modules:

### 1. Overview Dashboard (`/` or `overview`)
- **Description**: Executive command dashboard showing high-level watershed health indices, rainfall summaries, active interventions, and recent evidence signals.
- **Audience**: Government Officers (Administrator, District Officer, Field Officer) and Public Users.
- **Route**: `/`

### 2. GIS Command Map (`/gis` or `gis`)
- **Description**: Geospatial map viewer powered by Leaflet and OpenStreetMap. Features layers for watershed polygons, drainage channels, water bodies, agricultural zones, and GPS markers for assets like check dams and farm ponds.
- **Audience**: Government & Public.
- **Route**: `/gis`

### 3. Geo-Coded Field Evidence (`/geo-images` or `evidence`)
- **Description**: Visual evidence registry containing GPS-verified photographs linked to specific assets, villages, capture timestamps, and condition evaluations.
- **Audience**: Government & Public.
- **Route**: `/geo-images`

### 4. Development Insights / Spatial Intelligence (`/development-insights` or `insights`)
- **Description**: AI and geospatial analytical module detecting problem clusters (erosion hot-spots, declining retention zones, vegetation recovery trends).
- **Audience**: Government Administrators and District Officers.
- **Route**: `/development-insights`

### 5. Field Operations & Inspections (`/inspections` or `operations`)
- **Description**: Operational queue of routine and urgent asset inspections, pending field verifications, and inspection drawers.
- **Audience**: Government Officers only.
- **Route**: `/inspections`

### 6. Field Observation Upload (`/field-observation` or `observation`)
- **Description**: Real-time field data capture form with device GPS lock, image upload/camera capture, structural status rating, and remarks submission.
- **Audience**: Field Officers and District Officers.
- **Route**: `/field-observation`

### 7. Temporal Before / After Analysis (`/before-after` or `before-after`)
- **Description**: Interactive split-slider tool allowing side-by-side visual comparison between pre-monsoon baseline and post-monsoon water surge imagery.
- **Audience**: Government & Public.
- **Route**: `/before-after`

### 8. Priority Interventions (`/priority-intervention` or `priority`)
- **Description**: Algorithmic decision support map ranking structures based on urgency, water deficit, and physical damage.
- **Audience**: Government Administrators and District Officers.
- **Route**: `/priority-intervention`

### 9. Risk Alerts & Monitoring (`/alerts` or `alerts`)
- **Description**: Severity-coded operational alerts (Critical, High, Medium, Low, Info) tracking asset anomalies.
- **Audience**: Government Officers.
- **Route**: `/alerts`

### 10. Reports & Decision Support (`/reports` or `reports`)
- **Description**: Automated CSV and PDF report generator for watershed health, risk registers, and spatial indicators.
- **Audience**: Government & Public.
- **Route**: `/reports`
