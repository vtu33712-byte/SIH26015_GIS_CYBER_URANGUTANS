# JAL IMPACT Platform Overview

## Purpose and Mission
JAL IMPACT is a state-of-the-art Geospatial Watershed Development Monitoring and Decision Support Platform. The platform bridges the gap between field-level ground observations and high-level geospatial intelligence. It empowers district administrators, field engineers, panchayat representatives, and public citizens to track watershed development progress, monitor water harvesting infrastructure, detect environmental anomalies, and plan evidence-based interventions.

## Key Platform Capabilities
1. **Interactive GIS Command Map**: Visualizes watershed boundaries, micro-watersheds, drainage networks, contour elevation lines, and individual water harvesting structures.
2. **Geo-Coded Field Evidence Gallery**: Ingests, verifies, and catalogs field photographs tagged with GPS coordinates, timestamps, and structural metadata.
3. **Temporal Before/After Analysis**: Provides interactive split-screen comparison tools to assess structural construction and post-monsoon water surge.
4. **Field Observation & Live Geo-Tagging**: Mobile-friendly interface for field officers to capture real-time GPS coordinates, evaluate water presence, silt accumulation, and structural integrity.
5. **Spatial Intelligence & Hotspot Analysis**: Automated spatial pattern recognition highlighting clusters of declining water retention, erosion vulnerabilities, and overdue inspections.
6. **Multi-Tier Role Access**: Role-based access control separating public transparency dashboards from secure government command operations.
7. **Jal-Bot AI Copilot**: Grounded AI assistant offering RAG-powered watershed insights, navigation assistance, and metric explanations.
