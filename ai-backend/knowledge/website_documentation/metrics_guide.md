# JAL IMPACT Dashboard Metrics & Scientific Indices Guide

### 1. Watershed Health Index (Score: 0 - 100)
- **Formula & Composition**: Calculated as a weighted composite of Water Status (25%), Vegetation Vigour (20%), Structural Condition (20%), Soil/Land Conservation (20%), and Field Evidence Recency (15%).
- **Interpretation**: 
  - 80 - 100: Healthy, resilient catchment.
  - 60 - 79: Moderate; routine maintenance and monitoring needed.
  - Below 60: Degraded; priority intervention required.

### 2. Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI)
- **Formula**: `NDVI = (NIR - Red) / (NIR + Red)`
- **Role in Watersheds**: Measures photosynthetic vigor and biomass density. Positive trends indicate successful afforestation, agroforestry, and improved soil moisture retention along drainage courses.

### 3. Normalized Difference Water Index (NDWI)
- **Formula**: `NDWI = (Green - NIR) / (Green + NIR)`
- **Role in Watersheds**: Accurately delineates open water bodies, farm ponds, check dam impoundments, and surface water expansion post-monsoon.

### 4. Soil Exposure & Bare Ground Fraction
- **Role**: Highlights vulnerable sloping areas susceptible to sheet and rill erosion during high-intensity rainfall events.

### 5. Water Retention Deficit (%)
- **Role**: Identifies check dams or percolation ponds that retain significantly less water compared to historical averages or expected catchment yields, indicating siltation, porous foundation, or spillway breach.
