# Temporal Change Detection & Before/After Comparison

## Concept of Temporal Analysis
Temporal change detection evaluates the same geographic location across different time steps to observe development progress, structural changes, and environmental responses.

## Key Before/After Comparisons in JAL IMPACT
1. **Structural Intervention Impact (e.g. Check Dam WHS-021 in Kovilur)**:
   - **Before (June 2026 / Pre-Intervention)**: Dry seasonal gully, severe downcutting, lack of surface water impoundment, desiccated soil.
   - **After (September 2026 / Post-Monsoon Surge)**: Robust masonry check dam with spillway, +92% water volume increase, sustained reservoir pool feeding secondary irrigation canals.
2. **Catchment Recovery (e.g. Farm Pond WHS-042 in East Sector)**:
   - **Before (Pre-Rain)**: Exposed bare soil with 36% erosion vulnerability.
   - **After (Post-Rain Recovery)**: Expanded water surface area (+38%), improved soil moisture, stabilized vegetative bunds.
